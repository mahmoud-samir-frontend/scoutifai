Information may be visible to authorized users in the same organization according to their role—for example, a coach may view assigned players, and authorized medical staff may access relevant medical records. We may also share limited information with hosting, storage, security, technical support, and communications providers that process it for us under appropriate obligations.

We may disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a merger, financing, acquisition, or transfer of the Service with appropriate notice and safeguards. We do not sell personal information or use sensitive sports or medical information for advertising.
